## Why prices are not imported yet

Official pricing pages may show promotional prices, per-seat rates, annual billing discounts, taxes, upgrades, product bundles, or geographic offers. Without a normalized plan/region/currency/billing-period model and review, comparing a number would be misleading.

## Evidence review procedure
1. Validate the URL and its vendor ownership.
2. Record a specific verifiable claim and copy the supporting section title (not full copyrighted text).
3. Store date checked and applicable plan, geography, billing cycle and currency.
4. Compare against independent platform documentation where the claim is operationally material.
5. Record reviewer identity and approval separately from source discovery before a claim influences a public recommendation.
6. Flag older sources for re-review rather than treating historical information as current.

## Explicit restrictions
No scraped affiliate parameters, promotional referrals, invented vendor approval, commission rates or generated reviews. Candidate source URLs are not affiliate links. The current recommendations still show uncertainty rather than a false personalized fit.
