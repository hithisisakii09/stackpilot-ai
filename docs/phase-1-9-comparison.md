# Phase 1.9 — Same-category comparisons (prelaunch)

## Delivered
- Choose a category (CRM, SEO or Hosting) and two distinct tools from that category.
- A browser-only comparison displays descriptive candidate records and explicitly identifies missing editorial verification, team fit, budget fit and pricing.
- The original static tables remain accessible as a no-JavaScript fallback.
- Candidate source counts mean *links logged for review*, not confirmed claims, endorsements, affiliate relationships or review completeness.
- Product research profiles explicitly distinguish source intake dates from editorial approval.
- Client rendering uses `textContent`, not HTML injection or third-party network calls.
- Read-only comparison logic is covered by unit tests for cross-category rejection, unverified-claim display, duplicate selection, referral independence and catalog immutability.
- Desktop and mobile Chromium acceptance now exercises selections, error handling and accessibility scanning.

## Boundaries
- No scores or rankings are generated by the comparison.
- No live pricing, currency conversions, verified feature comparisons or claims of suitability.
- No authentication, registration, analytics, cookies, customer information collection, affiliate URL activation, merge or production promotion.
- Site pages retain `noindex` directives and hosting `X-Robots-Tag` restrictions on the development branch.

## Future evidence and editorial gates
1. Record authoritative source claims scoped to a named product version/plan, region, billing cadence and observation date.
2. Confirm terms and restrictions using vendor documentation; reconcile conflicts and stale evidence.
3. Perform a human editorial approval for any feature or pricing claim before presenting it as verified.
4. Ensure commercial arrangements never affect ordering or claims.
5. Complete protected remote-browser QA and review the actual deployment's headers.
6. Obtain separate permission to publish, remove indexing restrictions or enable monetization.
