## Source-to-publication workflow (each stage has a hard boundary)
1. **RESEARCH CANDIDATE** (automatic): prepare questions, first-party links, uncertainty and fictional examples. No public claims.
2. **SOURCE RECHECK** (automatic): record current provider URL/title/update date and exact plan/edition/region caveats. Do not impersonate independent product testing.
3. **ORIGINAL UTILITY QA** (automatic): check fictional sample integrity, content usability, accessibility, mobile rendering and links on private development previews.
4. **EDITORIAL SIGNOFF** (owner): verify statement by statement, attribution/licensing, tested vs untested, date, author and disclaimers. Without this stage, keep unpublished.
5. **LEGAL/PUBLISHER GATE** (owner): truthful identity/contact, privacy provider behavior, applicable disclosures and commercial hosting evidence.
6. **PUBLICATION GATE** (owner): separate explicit public-release authorization, deployment SHA, private-to-public visibility/indexing. No autonomous override.
7. **POST-LAUNCH MEASUREMENT** (only after approved): use genuine first-party analytics/search-console evidence if lawfully configured; record impressions, sessions and relevant clicks. Do not infer revenue from estimated search volumes or fabricate traffic.
8. **AFFILIATE ACTIVATION** (separate, never implied): partner approval, appropriate disclosures and owner authorization needed for each program.
