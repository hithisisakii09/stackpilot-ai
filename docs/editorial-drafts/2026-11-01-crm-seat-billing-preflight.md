### Synthetic arithmetic (fictional, not observed)

- **Unused capacity:** an imaginary Pipedrive team has **8 billed seats** and **5 active users**, with 3 vacant seats. Seat utilization is `5 / 8 = 62.5%`. This illustrates a *possible* seat audit, not a refund entitlement. The bill impact is **UNKNOWN** without the contract, plan and effective date.
- **Hiring:** a fictional account has 5 filled seats and no vacancy. Inviting a sixth Deals user may create a sixth billed seat according to the cited Pipedrive documentation. Do not compute a charge without the actual invoice and cycle.
- **Different role mixes:** an imaginary HubSpot team has 6 people, of whom 2 only need to view dashboards. Whether those two can use View-Only seats and which paid seat types the other four require depends on account model, role actions and permissions. **Do not** assert “4 paid seats” without account review.

**Buyer preflight checklist:** reconcile the last invoice against billed seats; list vacant seats; confirm whether a departing user's data and ownership are reassigned; verify each remaining person's required actions; confirm seat-removal timing in the actual subscription; save a dated, private quote before renewing or migrating. Never upload a real roster or invoice to StackPilot.
