## HubSpot — record exports

Source: [Export your records](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/import-and-export/export-records), displayed last updated 2026-04-02.

- The source describes current record property and association exports, requires Export permission, and points activity exports to separate paths.
- Default association exports include up to 1,000 associated record IDs per association column; CSV offers an all-associated-records option.
- Download links expire after 30 days; export history remains in the log for three years.
- The source describes a rolling limit of 300 exports per 24 hours and three simultaneous exports. Large CSV/XLSX exports above 1,000,000 rows may split into multiple files; XLS and XLSX have different column limits.
- Buyer caveat: Do not describe one export as a complete activity/history backup. Confirm object availability, user permissions, association scope and activity export in the actual buyer account.

## Pipedrive — spreadsheet imports

Source: [Importing data into Pipedrive with spreadsheets](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/importing-data-into-pipedrive-with-spreadsheets), displayed last updated 2026-10-19.

- The source describes XLS, XLSX and CSV imports, one worksheet/tab, a header row, a maximum of 200,000 rows and a file size below 50 MB.
- A correct global import permission is required. The documented entity types include people, organizations, leads, deals, projects, activities, notes and products.
- Required fields and mapping affect the import. The source says only a global admin can revert an import, within a 48-hour window.
- Buyer caveat: These are import limits, not export limits. No buyer plan, account role, regional entitlement or actual import was tested.

## Ahrefs — Site Audit crawl credits

Sources: [How Site Audit crawl credits are spent](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/3119402-how-are-crawl-credits-in-site-audit-spent) and [About Ahrefs Starter plan](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/9419051-about-ahrefs-starter-plan), both displayed dated 2026-09-03.

- The documentation says internal HTML pages returning HTTP 200 consume Site Audit crawl credits; redirects, 4xx/5xx responses, resources and external URLs do not.
- It states that the first 5,000 credits per verified project per month are free across plans.
- The Starter plan table separately distinguishes verified-project credits and unverified-project allowances (Free 0; Starter 500 pages / 10,000 credits; Lite 25,000 pages / 100,000 credits). Shared account credits are a separate row.
- Buyer caveat: Confirm the exact subscription, project ownership-verification state and shared account credits. Do not imply a buyer's site is verified or eligible for the free project allowance.

## Semrush — Site Audit limits and scope

Sources: [Site Audit limits](https://www.semrush.com/kb/31-site-audit) and [Configuring Site Audit](https://www.semrush.com/kb/539-configuring-site-audit), reopened 2026-11-03. No on-page last-updated date was visible.

- The help pages distinguish monthly page budgets, per-audit page caps and concurrent audits.
- The captured table states monthly/per-audit limits of 100/100 pages without an SEO Toolkit, Pro 100,000/20,000, Guru 300,000/20,000 and Business 1,000,000/100,000; concurrent audit limits are 1/2/2/5 respectively.
- JavaScript rendering is stated for Guru or Business SEO Toolkit. Semrush One Starter / Pro+ / Advanced are mapped in the source to Pro / Guru / Business SEO Toolkit limits.
- Domain scope includes subdomains and subfolders by default unless narrowed.
- Buyer caveat: Recheck subscription naming, plan mapping and current limits at publication time; no subscription or audit was operated.

## Kinsta — WordPress hosting overages

Source: [WordPress hosting plan overages](https://kinsta.com/docs/billing/wordpress-hosting-plans/overages/), displayed last updated 2026-03-09.

- The page describes plan meters for monthly visits or server bandwidth, with separate SSD and CDN allowances.
- Published examples include $0.50 per 1,000 visit overage, $0.50 per GB server-bandwidth overage, SSD at $2 per GB/month prorated daily and CDN at $0.05 per GB.
- It describes alerts at 80% and 100%, plus a 10% visit-overage alert. Upgrades are not retroactive; the page describes earlier payment or upgrade requests and temporary access limits for extreme overages.
- Buyer caveat: These are vendor-published examples, not a current buyer quote or account invoice. Confirm plan family, billing cycle, custom terms, taxes and account settings.

## Cloudways — off-site backup charges

Source: [Charges for off-site backups](https://support.cloudways.com/en/articles/5119648-charges-for-off-site-backups), dated 2025-11-11.

- The page describes Flexible backup storage at $0.033/GB, rounded in $0.50 increments, and states that Autonomous includes backup costs.
- Retention and changed data affect storage use.
- Buyer caveat: This is a backup-storage component, not the complete hosting bill. It does not establish total server pricing, regional taxes or every plan charge; recheck the source before publication.
