Before public use, owner or an authorized reviewer must verify:
1. Actual Netlify account settings: logs and retention, visitor IP handling, regions, subprocessors, DPA/data transfer terms where applicable, and contact/privacy inquiry mechanism.

3. Whether the actual production app stores or transmits input; source tests and deployed browser inspections should agree.

5. Data deletion/subject requests procedure and correct public contact channel, without posting personal identifiers into GitHub.
