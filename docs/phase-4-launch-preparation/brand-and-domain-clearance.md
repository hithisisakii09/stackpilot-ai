## 1. Brand Identity Evaluation: "ToolChoice Lab"

The repository currently uses **ToolChoice Lab** as a provisional publisher working name, pending trademark, corporate identity, and owner clearance.

### 1.1 Brand Positioning Principles
- **Editorial Neutrality:** The name must convey rigorous research, laboratory benchmarking, and independence rather than marketing hype or paid ranking ("BestTools", "TopSaaS", etc.).
- **Commercial Distinction:** Clear separation between editorial tools/guides and third-party SaaS vendors.
- **Publisher Entity:** Must be owned by an identified, legally responsible person or company before any affiliate applications or public launch.

### 1.2 Clearance Checklist (Owner-Only)
- [ ] **Trademark Search:** Check national trademark registers (e.g. USPTO TESS, EUIPO, Indian IP India) for conflicts with "ToolChoice" and "StackPilot".
- [ ] **Domain Availability:** Evaluate potential `.com` / `.org` / `.ai` domains without purchasing.
- [ ] **Corporate Registration:** Determine whether operation will proceed as an individual proprietorship or legal corporate entity.
- [ ] **Contact Email:** Provision an official domain-based email (e.g. `editor@...` or `privacy@...`) before submitting publisher applications.
